Privacy notice

This site is run by rotwurm, a music project based in Helsinki, Finland. It exists to share music — it collects as little about you as possible.

Analytics & advertising

This site uses the Meta Pixel and Conversions API (Meta Platforms Ireland Ltd) to measure visits and clicks on music platform links, and to improve the relevance of our ads on Facebook and Instagram. This involves cookies (such as _fbp) and technical data like your IP address and browser type being shared with Meta. Meta's own processing is described in the Meta Privacy Policy.

We also use Cloudflare Web Analytics (Cloudflare, Inc.) to count page views and visits. It is cookieless and does not track you across sites; see the Cloudflare Privacy Policy.

We don't collect names, emails, or accounts here. We only see aggregate statistics — never you personally.

Your choices

You can control ad personalisation in your Facebook ad preferences, block cookies in your browser settings, or use a content blocker — the site works fine either way.

Third-party embeds

Pages on this site embed players from Spotify, SoundCloud, and YouTube. When they load, those services may set their own cookies under their own privacy policies.
